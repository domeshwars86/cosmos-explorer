Dwarf Planets

Dwarf planets orbit the Sun and are massive enough to be rounded by their own gravity, but — unlike the eight major planets — they have not gravitationally cleared their orbital neighborhood of other debris. Pluto was reclassified from a planet to a dwarf planet by the International Astronomical Union in 2006.
