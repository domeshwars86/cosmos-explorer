Worlds Around Other Stars

Exoplanets

Planets are found using indirect methods, since they're far too faint and close to their stars to photograph directly in most cases.

Transit

Detects a tiny dip in starlight as a planet passes in front of its star.

Radial Velocity

Detects the star's slight wobble caused by a planet's gravity.

Direct Imaging

Directly photographs a planet, usually a young, large, widely-separated world.

Microlensing

Detects a brightness spike as a planet's gravity bends light from a background star.
