The Galactic Center

Sagittarius A*

CONCEPTUAL VISUAL

A supermassive black hole roughly 4 million times the mass of our Sun sits at the center of the Milky Way — confirmed by decades of astronomers tracking stars orbiting an invisible, immensely massive object.

Our Solar System orbits from roughly 26,000 light-years away — an immense, safe distance from Sagittarius A*.
